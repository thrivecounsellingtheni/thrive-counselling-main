About us

Thrive Counselling and Career Guidance Centre is passionate about helping individuals overcome behavioural and emotional difficulties.

At Thrive, we provide specialized services that support personal growth and help address emotional, behavioural, and relationship-related challenges. We work with children, adolescents, young adults, and families to resolve psychological issues and improve their quality of life.

Thrive offers a wide range of psychological services, including psychotherapy, school-based programs, skill development programs, and training programs for women and children. We also provide guidance and support to parents and teachers through workshops and seminars.

Vision

  • Thrive Counselling and Career Guidance Centre's vision is for every individual, family, and youth to reach their full potential and realize their dreams in a compassionate and caring environment.
  • Thrive strives to enhance the mental health and the overall well-being of family members in the community and to help people transform personal challenges into life-enhancing opportunities.

Mission

  • Our mission is to build and support emotionally strong individuals, families, and community through confidential, affordable, and transformative counselling, education and advocacy.
  • To provide counselling services to children, adolescents, adults, couples, families and senior adults from diverse backgrounds and varying socio-economic contexts.
  • To empower individuals, families, and youth to take control of their destiny and function productively.
  • The centre facilitates this by working with clients collaboratively to equip them with the necessary skills to realize their dreams, through counselling, education, and social skills training.
  • Services are delivered in a caring and friendly environment with a personal touch, and at rates that are affordable for everyone.

Core Values

Humanity

Thrive believes that counselling should be accessible to whoever needs it regardless of financial situation. Counselling is meant to prevent and alleviate human suffering. It promotes peace within and amongst all people.

Respect

Thrive CCGC believes that humans are born with a good heart and moral power. Every individual is teachable, improvable, and changeable.

Diversity & Inclusiveness

The centre strives to create a safe place for everyone to share their life journey, regardless of race, ethnicity, age and gender.

Integrity

Thrive CCGC strongly uphold moral and ethical principles in our daily practice and in delivery of services. Our clients entrust their wellness to our professional care and expect to be treated, and to receive treatment, in a manner that honours professional codes and standards.

Caring, Concern and Compassion

Thrive embrace and uphold these attributes throughout our practice of service as our clients' well-being is foremost in the minds and hearts of everyone.
