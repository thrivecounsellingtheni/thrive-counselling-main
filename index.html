Our Psychologist

V.R. Vanajaa

V.R. Vanajaa is a highly experienced Counselling Psychologist and Founder of Thrive Counselling and Career Guidance Centre, offering compassionate support for individuals, couples, children, and families through professional therapy and guidance.

About V.R. Vanajaa

V.R. Vanajaa is a certified Counselling Psychologist, Professional Social Worker, and the Founder of Thrive Counselling and Career Guidance Centre in the Theni District, Tamil Nadu.

With over 25 years of experience in community development as a Social Worker and 10 years of exclusive practice as a Counselling Psychologist, she brings compassionate, professional support to individuals, couples, and families.

Education & Qualifications

  • M.Sc. in Psychology
  • Master of Social Work (MSW)
  • Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)
  • Specialized Certifications: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT - Levels I & II), Family Therapy, and Child Psychology.

Areas of Expertise

She has extensive experience helping clients navigate a wide range of challenges, including:

  • Counseling: Individual, Pre & Post-Marital, Relationship, and Domestic Violence Counseling.
  • Therapy: Child & Adolescent Therapy, Behavioral Therapy, Parenting Guidance, and De-addiction Therapy.
  • Mental Health Support: Therapy for Depression, Anxiety, OCPD, and Procrastination.
  • Specialized Support: Peer-relationship issues (school/college) and supportive therapy for children with Autism.

Working Style & Approach

"I work one-on-one with individuals as well as with groups. My aim is to empower my clients to effectively deal with life's challenges, helping them build resilience, overcome obstacles, and improve their self-esteem."

How to Connect

Psycho-education and psychotherapy sessions are available by prior appointment through multiple convenient modes:

  • In-Person (at the center)
  • Audio Call / Phone Call
  • Chat & Video Consultation
  • Languages Spoken: Fluent in Tamil and English.
